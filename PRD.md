# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

# Fuel Prep Landing Page

## Product Overview

Fuel Prep is a meal-planning platform designed to simplify healthy eating for students, busy professionals, and fitness enthusiasts. The platform helps users reach their fitness goals through personalized meal plans, AI-generated meal recommendations, simple meal prep recipes, and budget-friendly grocery lists.

The landing page serves as the first introduction to the Fuel Prep brand. Its primary purpose is to educate visitors about the product, communicate its value proposition, and collect lead information from potential users.

---

# Product Vision

Fuel Prep makes meal planning easy.

Users should leave the website understanding that Fuel Prep removes the complexity from healthy eating by providing personalized meal recommendations and grocery planning in a simple, affordable, and convenient way.

---

# Business Goal

The goal of the landing page is to:

* Introduce the Fuel Prep concept
* Communicate key product benefits
* Generate interest in the platform
* Collect leads through a Get Started form
* Build an audience before product launch
