About FuelPrep

FuelPrep was created to help students and young adults eat healthier without spending too much time or money. The platform is designed for people who want to build muscle, lose fat, or maintain a healthy lifestyle while staying on a budget.

By combining meal planning, macro tracking, recipe ideas, and grocery budgeting, FuelPrep makes nutrition more simple, affordable, and realistic.
